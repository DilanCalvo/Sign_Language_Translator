How to use Signex

Set up your scene

  • Light your hands from the front. A window or lamp facing you beats ceiling light; avoid strong backlight.
  • Keep the background simple and steady — a plain wall works best for the detector.
  • Frame yourself from the head to the waist. In Words mode your shoulders must be visible — signs are read relative to your body.
  • Stand about 0.5–1.5 m (2–5 ft) from the camera, with the whole hand inside the frame.

Signing

  • Letters / Numbers: hold each static pose steady until it locks in — the corner readout shows what the model sees and how sure it is (green means committed). Pause, or tap SPACE, to end a word.
  • Words: sign naturally with one or both hands. The recognized sign is spoken and added to the conversation. Pause to start a new sentence.
  • If the readout shows top 3 guesses, the model is unsure — adjust your hand angle or lighting.

Talking back

  • The hearing side types (or dictates) in the conversation panel; the message pops up large over the camera so the signer can read it instantly.
  • Voice, chosen voice and letter-speech behavior live in Settings.

Recording

  • Press Rec to start keeping a transcript — it follows you across all three modes. Export downloads it as TXT and CSV.

Performance tips

  • A recent phone or a laptop gives the smoothest experience; close other heavy tabs/apps if recognition feels slow.
  • Chrome, Edge or Safari are recommended. Everything runs locally — no video ever leaves your device.
